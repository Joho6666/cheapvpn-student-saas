Troubleshooting & FAQ

wifi_off Cannot connect to any server?

Ensure your antivirus or firewall is not blocking CheapVPN. Try switching protocols in the settings menu.

speed Connection is slow?

Try connecting to a server geographically closer to you. Look for servers with the lowest ping bars.
